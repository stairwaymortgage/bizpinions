Key features we tested

Payroll processing

Once set up, running payroll takes under 5 minutes. Gusto walks you through hours, any changes, and confirms before processing. Direct deposit hits within 2 business days on Simple, next day on Plus.

Automated tax filing

This is Gusto's strongest feature. Federal, state, and local payroll taxes are calculated, withheld, and filed automatically — including W-2s and 1099s at year end. For most small businesses this eliminates the need for a payroll accountant.

Employee self-service

New employees receive an email invite and fill in their own bank details, tax withholding, and emergency contacts. You never manually type their information. This alone saves an hour per new hire.
