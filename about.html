Why we built this

The internet is full of review sites that bury the answer at the bottom of a 3,000-word article. They do it deliberately — more words means more ad impressions, more time on page, more clicks. The reader suffers so the site can make an extra dollar.

We built Bizpinions on a different principle: give you the answer in the first paragraph. If you want the proof, it's right below. But you shouldn't have to wade through 2,000 words of context-setting to find out what we actually think.

"The answer is always in the first paragraph. We tell you what we think before we tell you why. That's the whole model."

Our editorial principles

01
Verdict first. Always.
Every article, review, and guide leads with the answer. No preamble, no burying the lede, no making you scroll to find out what we think.
02
Honest limitations included.
Every review includes what doesn't work, who it's wrong for, and what you should use instead. A review with no cons is an ad, not a review.
03
Affiliate links are disclosed.
We earn commissions when you buy through our links. This is disclosed on every single page. Our opinions are never influenced by who pays us — if a product is bad, we say it's bad.
04
Sponsored placements are labeled.
Companies can pay to be reviewed and featured on Bizpinions. When they do, it's clearly labeled as a sponsored placement. The scores and verdicts are ours, not theirs.
05
We cover every stage.
Start it, Fund it, Run it, Buy it, Sell it. Every stage of a business journey has its own section. We cover the whole lifecycle — not just the flashy startup stuff.

How we make money

Bizpinions makes money in four ways, all disclosed:

Affiliate commissions — when you click a link and buy software or a service, we earn a commission. Disclosed on every page.

Sponsored placements — companies pay to have their product reviewed and featured. Labeled as sponsored on every review.

Newsletter sponsorships — one sponsor per weekly email issue. Always labeled.

Google AdSense — display ads on some pages. Standard stuff.
